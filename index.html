01About

Floukie (Alexander Parnell) is an independent researcher, builder, and entrepreneur working across AI, mathematics, and computational systems. His papers are published open access with permanent DOIs via Zenodo under the name Alexander Parnell.

He is co-founder of Vektorgeist with his uncle Lee Kersh, where the research programme is put into practice as local-first AI tooling and agent-governance software.
