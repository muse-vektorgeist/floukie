About

Floukie (Alexander Parnell) is an independent researcher working on local-first AI, agent governance, machine self-models, and relational ontology. His papers are published open access with permanent DOIs via Zenodo under the name Alexander Parnell. He is co-founder of Vektorgeist with his uncle Lee Kersh.

Vektorgeist

Vektorgeist builds local-first AI tools and agent-governance software, including the Aviary suite of agent infrastructure. Its research programme covers relational ontology, machine self-models, and local-first AI, and is documented at the Vektorgeist research index.
