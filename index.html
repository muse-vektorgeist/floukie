About

Floukie (Alexander Parnell) is an independent researcher working on local-first AI, agent governance, machine self-models, and relational ontology. His papers are published open access with permanent DOIs via Zenodo under the name Alexander Parnell.

He is co-founder of Vektorgeist with his uncle Lee Kersh, where the research programme is put into practice as local-first AI tooling and agent-governance software.
